Migration Path: Virginia to Alabama

Tracing the Jones/Anderson/Donnell/Perkins network from Louisa and Bedford counties, Virginia to Madison County, Alabama and on to Tuscaloosa — each verified waypoint carrying its source, and speculative links marked as such.

How to read this map. This is a schematic diagram, not a geographic map — node positions show relative order (Virginia → Alabama), not real distances or coordinates. A solid green node is a verified waypoint; a dashed amber node is under review — an area of interest with no sourced stop plotted. Solid lines are documented movements; dashed lines are speculative links. Click any node for its sourced detail.

Waypoints, with sources

Map data & sources

  • Louisa County, Virginia, "Register of marriages, Louisa County, Virginia, 1766-1861," DGS 007578998 (film 32190), image 61 of 527 — signed-in FamilySearch read, 2026-09-23 ("Llewellin Jones" to "Mary Anderson," October 22, 1792; consent of Nelson Anderson father; surety Alexander Anderson; witnesses Martin Baker, Andrew Thomson).
  • Bureau of Land Management, General Land Office (BLM GLO) — Lewellen Jones's seven November 20, 1818 patents (Credit Volume, Harrison Land Act of April 15, 1800), Huntsville Meridian, Madison County, Alabama.
  • Hubbs, "On the Razor's Edge," Alabama Heritage (Fall 2020) — Shandy Wesley Jones in Tuscaloosa by 1837, free Black barber, Tuscaloosa County legislator 1868–70 (via Hoksbergen & DeNeefe, Alabama Review 79(3), July 2026).
  • Counter-Archive research notes — Bedford County chancery cases (Bedford 019) queued for the next read batch; no waypoint claimed there yet.
